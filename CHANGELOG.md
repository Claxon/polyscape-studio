# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- One dialog with a large live preview on the left and the controls on the right. The dialog fits the screen, can be resized, and remembers its size. **Cancel / Apply / OK** sit at the top of the controls so they're always visible.
- Preview: drag shapes to move them, Shift+drag to scale, drag the white dot to rotate. Wheel over the image to zoom (including below *Fit*), drag an empty area to pan. *Preview* and *Before* views.
- 43 shapes, which can be nested: child shapes move with their parent and are clipped to it.
- Content transforms: offset, rotate, zoom, flip, mirror half, kaleidoscope, twirl, pixelate, blur, RGB split.
- Colour filters: brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, greyscale, sepia, invert, posterize, threshold, grain, tint, duotone. Gradient/glass overlay.
- 24 blend modes, outline, shadow/glow, repeat (echo) copies.
- 20 built-in presets, a randomiser, saved presets, undo/redo.
- **Apply** / **OK** add the result as a new *Polyscape* layer — the full image, or the shapes only on a transparent layer.
- One-click Windows installer.
